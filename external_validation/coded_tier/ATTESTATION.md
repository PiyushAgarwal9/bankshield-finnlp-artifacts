# Attestation — coded-tier human validation

The two coded-tier columns (expert_A, expert_B in coded_validation_results.json; completed forms
coded_form_expertA.xlsx / coded_form_expertB.xlsx) were each independently completed by a separate
human domain expert, blind to the answer key, with no AI generation or AI assistance. The authors
received the completed sheets from the experts and filed them into the repository; the released form
copies were therefore re-saved once by an author account and then anonymized (author metadata removed;
originals retained privately). Personal identifiers were not collected on the forms (the "About you"
fields are intentionally blank). The experts participated with informed consent to anonymous aggregate
reporting and received no compensation.

Verifiable independence evidence (see score_coded_validation.py): the two experts disagree with our
labels on 8 and 10 of the 80 items respectively, and with each other on 4 items (Cohen's k=0.90);
neither reproduces the answer key. Per-expert professional metadata was not recorded on the forms; the
above is an author attestation.
